🥗 Daily Meal Plan
Daily Targets: 1,800-1,900 calories | 130-145g protein | 60-75g fat | 150-180g carbs
Breakfast
400 cal • 35g protein
  • 3 egg whites + 1 whole egg scrambled with spinach
  • 1/2 cup oatmeal with berries
  • Black coffee or tea
Mid-Morning Snack
200 cal • 20g protein
  • Greek yogurt (5.3oz, 0-2% fat) + 1 tbsp almond butter
  • OR protein shake with banana
Lunch
500 cal • 40g protein
  • 5-6 oz grilled chicken, salmon, or lean turkey
  • Large mixed greens salad with olive oil vinaigrette
  • 1/2 cup quinoa or sweet potato
  • Assorted vegetables
Afternoon Snack
150 cal • 15g protein
  • String cheese + apple
  • OR 1/4 cup hummus with veggie sticks
Dinner
500 cal • 35g protein
  • 5-6 oz protein (steak, fish, pork tenderloin, tofu)
  • Roasted vegetables (Brussels sprouts, asparagus, cauliflower)
  • Side salad with vinaigrette
  • 1/3 cup brown rice (training days only)
Evening (Optional)
150 cal • 10g protein
  • Cottage cheese with cucumber
  • OR casein protein shake
🌟 Perimenopause Nutrition Tips
  • Front-load carbs earlier in the day
  • Keep healthy fats at 25-30% of calories
  • Stay hydrated: 80-100 oz water daily
  • Consider creatine: 5g daily (great for women 40+)
  • Limit wine to 1-2 glasses/week during this 3-month push
